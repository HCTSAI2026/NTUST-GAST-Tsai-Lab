Research

Wearable ECG Hydrogel Nanocomposites

Development of soft, skin-compatible conductive hydrogel nanocomposites for accurate and stable ECG monitoring. The platform emphasizes long-term attachment, movement tolerance and integration with flexible electronics.

Sprayable Thermosensitive Hydrogels

Thermal-sensitive sprayable hydrogel systems designed for minimally invasive application, including postoperative anti-adhesion barriers and rapid hemostatic coverage over irregular surfaces.

Microbead-Based Bioseparation

Engineered microbeads for selective isolation and capture of nanoscale biological targets from complex samples, supporting diagnostic and biomarker applications.

COF-Integrated Ionic Membranes

Integration of covalent organic frameworks into ionic conducting membranes to tune ion transport, stability and selectivity for energy, separation and sensing systems.

Hydrogel & Biomaterials

Injectable, adhesive and responsive polymer networks for local delivery, tissue interaction and regenerative medicine.

Polymer Nanocomposites

Functional nanocomposite materials with controllable mechanical, interfacial and transport properties.
