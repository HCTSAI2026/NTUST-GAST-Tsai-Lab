RESEARCH THEMES

Research

Wearable ECG Hydrogel Nanocomposites

Soft, skin-compatible conductive hydrogel nanocomposites for stable ECG monitoring, long-term attachment and flexible electronics.

Sprayable Thermosensitive Hydrogels

Thermal-sensitive sprayable hydrogel systems for minimally invasive application, postoperative anti-adhesion barriers and hemostatic coverage.

Microbeads for Bioseparation & Detection

Functional microbead platforms for selective capture and recovery of nanoscale biological targets from complex samples.

COF-Integrated Ionic Membranes

COF-integrated ionic membranes for controlled ion transport, energy, separation and sensing systems.

Functional Polymers & Theranostic Nanoparticles

Functional polymer shells and nanocomposite architectures integrating targeting, imaging, therapeutic payloads and stimuli-responsive release.
