LABORATORY CAPABILITIES

Facilities & Instruments

Our laboratory integrates particle characterization, optical spectroscopy, chromatographic analysis, rheology, surface analysis, and advanced fluorescence imaging to support research in functional polymers, hydrogels, particles, membranes, biomaterials, and drug-delivery systems.

Major Analytical Instruments

9 instruments
Anton Paar · Litesizer DLS 501

Dynamic Light Scattering Analyzer

Particle-size and colloidal characterization for nanoparticles, polymer assemblies, micelles, and microspheres.

LabTech

Chromatographic Analysis System

Automated chromatographic platform for polymer and formulation analysis, including oil-phase GPC-related workflows.

JASCO · FP-8250

Spectrofluorometer

Fluorescence excitation and emission measurements for probes, nanoparticles, biomaterials, and drug-delivery studies.

JASCO · HPLC System

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography

Separation and quantitative analysis of small molecules, drugs, degradation products, and formulation components.

JASCO · NRS-4500

Raman Spectrometer

Raman spectroscopy and chemical mapping for polymers, membranes, particles, composites, and functional materials.

JASCO · V-730

UV–Visible Spectrophotometer

Absorbance and optical characterization for polymers, nanoparticles, drug formulations, and solution-based assays.

Leica

Inverted Fluorescence Microscope

Fluorescence imaging for cell-based studies, biomaterial interfaces, cellular uptake, and fluorescently labeled samples.

TA Instruments · HR30

Rheometer

Viscoelasticity, flow behavior, gelation, and temperature-dependent rheological characterization of hydrogels and polymers.

Surface Characterization

Contact Angle Analyzer

Surface wettability and interfacial characterization for membranes, coatings, polymer films, and functional surfaces.

1 instrument

ELISA Microplate Reader

Microplate-based absorbance and fluorescence measurements for ELISA, cell viability assays, protein quantification, and other cell-based biomedical experiments.
