TAIWAN TECH · GIAST

Functional Polymers, Hydrogels & Advanced Membranes

We develop functional and porous polymer systems—including smart hydrogels, micro- and nanoparticles, and COF/COP-based materials—for localized drug delivery, theranostics, tissue repair, bioseparation, and wearable bioelectronics. In parallel, we engineer ion-conductive and selective membranes for fuel cells, redox-flow batteries, electrolysis, and advanced separation technologies.

Research Highlights

Sprayable Thermosensitive Hydrogels

Minimally invasive hydrogel systems for tissue coverage, anti-adhesion and hemostatic applications.

Hydrogel & COF Drug Delivery Systems

Localized and controlled delivery platforms integrating smart hydrogels with porous COF-based materials.

COF Ionic Membranes

Ordered ion-transport pathways for energy conversion and biomedical sensing.
